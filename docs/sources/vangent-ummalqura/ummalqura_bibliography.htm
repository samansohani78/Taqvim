Web Sources

  • Umm al-Qura Calendar: The Umm al-Qura calendar as developed and computed by the KACST (available in Arabic, Chinese, English, French, Indonesian, Malaysian, Turkish and Urdu).
  • Hilal Sighting in Saudi Arabia – A First Hand Report: An account of the Umm al-Qura calendar as it was observed in Saudi Arabia in 1416-1421 AH (1995-2000 CE) by Salman Zafar Shaikh.
  • Haj Date Change Leads to Confusion (article from Arab News, 17 January 2005).
  • The Actual Saudi Dating System (on the website of the Islamic Crescents’ Observation Project).
  • The History of the UK Following the Saudi Arabia Moonsighting Decision: An insightful account by Mufti Muhammad Yusuf Danka.
  • Saudi Moon Sighting Reports – A Review: An astronomical analysis of lunar crescent sighting reports from Saudi Arabia by Mahmood Jawaid.
  • Sky View Café: Convenient online astronomy applet for determining the times of sunset, moonset and the appearance of the lunar crescent at Mecca (or any other location on Earth).
  • Predicting the First Visibility of the Lunar Crescent: Accurate astronomical predictions for the first visibility of the lunar crescent with global visibility maps from 1432 AH to 1440 AH (2010 to 2019).
  • Aramco World Hijri Calendars: Printable calendars based on the Umm al-Qura calendar (from 2005 onwards) can be downloaded from the Aramco World website for the years:

    • Gregorian-Hijri Calendar for 2008 (1428/1429/1430 AH)
    • Gregorian-Hijri Calendar for 2009 (1430/1431 AH)
    • Gregorian-Hijri Calendar for 2010 (1431/1432 AH)
    • Gregorian-Hijri Calendar for 2011 (1432/1433 AH)
    • Gregorian-Hijri Calendar for 2012 (1433/1434 AH)

    • Gregorian-Hijri Calendar for 2018 (1439/1440 AH)
    • Gregorian-Hijri Calendar for 2019 (1440/1441 AH)
  • Qibla Plus: Islamic app (qibla, prayer times, Islamic calendar) in several languages for the iPhone and iPod which allows the conversion of Western calendar dates into various Islamic calendars, including the Umm al-Qura calendar.
